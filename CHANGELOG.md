# Changelog

All notable changes to SER Player are listed here.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-26

### What's new

- **First public release**, published by Vesperis Labs under CC BY 4.0.
- **About window.** Open it from the ⋮ menu to see the version, the publisher and the license. The license and third-party notices are now installed next to the app.
- The keyboard shortcuts and "make SER Player the default" windows match the rest of the app.

### Fixes

- The mouse pointer no longer stays hidden in menus and windows opened while a video is playing.
